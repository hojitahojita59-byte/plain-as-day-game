with its licence. The rule of the project: **art and sound must be CC0**; fonts
may be SIL OFL, which allows free use in any game as long as the licence travels
with the font.

## Made for the project

The characters (box figures, their pixel cloth and their vector faces in
`art/actors/caras/`, generated by `herramientas/generar_caras.js`), the
street, rectory and cemetery layouts, the shaders and all the code were made for
*Plain as Day*.
